# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

*   Using welcoming and inclusive language.
*   Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
*   Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
*   Focusing on what is best for the users of the software.
*   Showing empathy towards other community members.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

*   Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
*   Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
    attacks.
*   Public or private harassment.
*   Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or
    electronic address, without explicit permission.
*   Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
    professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, code review) and whenever someone represents the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a
[private report](https://github.com/simplemind121/odoo19-barcode-ce/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the repository owner,
[@simplemind121](https://github.com/simplemind121), directly on GitHub.
Reports are reviewed and investigated, and the reporter's identity is kept
confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
